Our Mission

AI API pricing is opaque, complex, and changes frequently. A developer building their first chatbot shouldn't need to wade through provider documentation tables, create custom spreadsheets, and manually calculate token costs just to budget their project.

AI Cost Calculator gives you a single, free, no-login tool that does all the calculation in your browser — instantly, accurately, and privately.

How It Works

Every calculation runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No data is sent to our servers. Projects you save are stored in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages as a static site — no backend, no database, no user accounts. This keeps things simple, fast, and private.

How We Stay Free

This site is supported by Google AdSense advertising. Ads appear below tools and in guide sections — never inside the calculator inputs or results, so they never interfere with your work.

We do not sell user data. We do not have user data — because we don't collect it.

Accuracy & Disclaimer

Pricing data is researched from official provider documentation and updated regularly. However, AI pricing changes frequently — always verify current rates with your provider before committing to a budget. All calculations are estimates for planning purposes only.
