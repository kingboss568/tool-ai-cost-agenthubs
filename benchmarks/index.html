Understanding the Benchmarks

MMLU

Massive Multitask Language Understanding. Tests general knowledge across 57 academic subjects including math, history, law, medicine, and science. Score represents accuracy (%). Best-in-class: ~89%.

HumanEval

OpenAI's code generation benchmark. Given a function docstring, the model must write correct Python code. Measured as pass@1 (%). Tests functional coding ability, not style. Best-in-class: ~94%.

GSM8K

Grade School Math 8,000 problems. Tests multi-step arithmetic word problems requiring logical reasoning. Measured as accuracy (%). Requires chain-of-thought reasoning to score high. Best-in-class: ~97%.

MT-Bench

Multi-Turn Benchmark. GPT-4 rates model responses to challenging two-turn conversations on a scale of 1–10. Tests instruction following, reasoning, and conversational quality. Best-in-class: ~9.3.

Arena Elo

LMSYS Chatbot Arena Elo rating. Derived from crowdsourced blind comparisons where users vote for the better response. More reliable than automated benchmarks for real-world quality. 1200+ is considered excellent.

Speed & Latency

Tokens per second (TPS) and milliseconds per output token. Higher TPS = faster generation. Lower latency = more responsive for streaming. These metrics vary significantly by provider load and region.

Performance vs Cost Analysis

Benchmark scores don't tell the whole story — cost efficiency matters. Here's how to think about the performance/cost tradeoff:

Top Performers (Highest Scores)

  • Claude 3.5 Sonnet — Highest HumanEval (93.7), top MT-Bench (9.2), and Arena Elo (1295). Best overall quality.
  • GPT-4o — Strong across all categories. Best balance of capability and speed at its tier.
  • Gemini 1.5 Pro — Strong MMLU and GSM8K, especially for long-context tasks where competitors don't even operate.

Best Value (Score per Dollar)

  • Gemini 1.5 Flash — Solid scores at $0.075/M input. Exceptional price-to-performance ratio.
  • GPT-4o Mini — 82% MMLU at 17× cheaper than GPT-4o. Remarkable efficiency for routine tasks.
  • Llama 3.1 70B — Near-frontier performance at open-source prices via Together AI or self-hosting.
Key insight: The quality gap between premium and economy models has shrunk dramatically in 2024. For many production use cases, an economy model achieves 90–95% of premium model quality at 5–10% of the cost. Run your own evaluation on representative samples before committing to a model tier.

Benchmark Limitations

Public benchmarks have important limitations to keep in mind when making model selection decisions:

  • Training data contamination: Some benchmarks may have leaked into training data, inflating scores. Models trained after benchmark release may have memorized answers.
  • Task-specific performance: A model with high MMLU may still perform poorly on your specific task. Always evaluate on your own use case before committing.
  • Evaluation methodology varies: Different research groups run the same benchmarks differently, producing varying results. Always check the methodology.
  • Benchmarks become outdated quickly: The AI field moves fast. Scores listed here reflect late 2024 results; newer model versions may score significantly higher.
  • Capability ≠ reliability: A model that scores 95% on HumanEval might fail 20% of the time on your specific coding style. Variance matters, not just average scores.
