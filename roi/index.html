How ROI is calculated

Monthly savings

Labor hours × hourly rate × automation percentage. This is how much you'd save by automating those tasks with AI.

Net monthly savings

Monthly labor savings minus AI subscription, maintenance, and training costs. This is your actual monthly benefit after all AI costs.

Payback period

One-time setup cost divided by net monthly savings. How many months until the AI implementation has paid for itself.
