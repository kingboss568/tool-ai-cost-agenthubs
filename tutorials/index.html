Case Study

Chatbot Cost Analysis

⏱ 8 min Intermediate

Follow along as we analyze the costs of a real customer support chatbot: 50K monthly users, $4,200/month initial spend reduced to $920/month through model switching and caching.

Study outline:

  1. Initial setup: GPT-4 Turbo, 50K users, no optimization
  2. Baseline cost measurement: $4,200/month
  3. Phase 1: Switch to GPT-4o — saves 67%
  4. Phase 2: Add prompt caching — saves additional 30%
  5. Phase 3: Route simple queries to GPT-4o Mini
  6. Final cost: $920/month — 78% reduction
  7. Quality evaluation: no detectable difference in user satisfaction
Case Study

Content Pipeline ROI

⏱ 10 min Intermediate

A marketing agency generates 1,000 articles/month. See how they combined Gemini Flash for drafts with Claude for editing, reducing time from 4 hours to 20 minutes per article and cutting content costs by 85%.

Study outline:

  1. Business context: 1,000 articles/month, 3-person writing team
  2. Before: manual writing at $150/article = $150K/month total cost
  3. Architecture: Gemini Flash for draft + Claude 3.5 Sonnet for polish
  4. Token analysis: ~3,000 tokens/article, $0.40 in AI costs
  5. Human review: reduced to 20 min/article at $12/hour
  6. New total cost: $4,000/month
  7. ROI calculation using the ROI Calculator

Code Assistant Budget Planning

⏱ 8 min Intermediate

A dev team of 20 uses Claude 3.5 Sonnet for code review on 200 PRs/month. Detailed cost analysis, time savings calculations, and build-vs-buy comparison for internal tooling.

Study outline:

  1. Setup: 20 devs, 10 PRs/dev/month, avg 500 lines of code changed

  5. ROI: 41× return on AI investment
  6. Build vs buy analysis: Copilot at $19/seat vs custom solution
