Understanding your usage pattern

Burstiness score

A score above 3 indicates high variance — your peak usage is more than 3x the average. This increases rate-limit risk and unpredictable costs.

Rate limit risk

Most providers apply per-minute or per-hour token rate limits. High-peak patterns are more likely to hit limits even if daily totals are within quota.

Load balancing

Moving workloads from peak to off-peak hours (often 2-6 AM) can reduce rate-limit failures, improve response times, and sometimes unlock lower pricing tiers.
